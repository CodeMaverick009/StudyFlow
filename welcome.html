Everything in one place

Tasks & materials

Add assignments, homework, tests and projects as they come in. Give each one a deadline, an estimated study time, a difficulty, and a priority, so you always know what matters most.

Planner

Turn your tasks into an actual schedule. See what's due each day on a calendar, and keep your workload spread out instead of piling up.

Study

When it's time to work, start a focused study session with a timer built around one task at a time — no tab-switching, no multitasking.

Progress

Your completed tasks and finished sessions turn into real insight — how much you studied, which subjects got the attention, and how consistent you've been.
